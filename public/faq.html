Frequently Asked Questions

Is my identity ever shown to a member?
No. Follow and like counts are public, but who followed or liked you is never shown to anyone — including you.
Who can actually reach me?
Only someone with your exact link or exact username. There's no public directory or browsing — you're never listed anywhere.
What does verification involve?
A quick ID check and a selfie, reviewed before your Room goes live. This keeps every Room on Xerelle genuine.
Can I set my own price?
Yes. You choose your monthly subscription price, starting from a ₦10,000 floor — go higher if you believe your access is worth more.
How do I get paid?
Payments are processed securely through Paystack. You keep the majority of every subscription, gift, and video call — full breakdown in our Terms.
What can I share in my Room?
Photos, a 24-hour Story to keep your Room active, and direct messages with your members. Video calls and gifting are on the way.
