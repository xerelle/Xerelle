This document is a working draft prepared ahead of formal legal review. It reflects our current, good-faith policy and applies as-is in the meantime.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Xerelle Technologies ("Xerelle," "we," "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our Platform. We are committed to complying with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable data protection law.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: username, display name, phone number, and email (optional for subscribers).

Verification information (creators only): a government-issued ID document and a live selfie, used solely to confirm identity and age before a creator profile is activated.

Content you provide: messages, Feed posts, Stories, and any media you upload.

Payment information: processed directly by our third-party payment provider. Xerelle does not store your full card number or banking credentials.

Usage information: basic activity data such as login times and feature usage, used to maintain and improve the Platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Verify creator identity and eligibility
  • Provide and maintain the Platform's features (messaging, Feed, Stories, subscriptions)
  • Process payments and payouts
  • Investigate reports of abuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • Communicate with you about your account

4. How We Protect Verification Data

Identity documents and selfies submitted for verification are stored securely and access is restricted to authorized review purposes only. This data is not displayed publicly and is not shared with other users.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Our payment processor, to complete transactions
  • Our infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud hosting), solely to operate the Platform
  • Law enforcement, if legally required to do so

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, security, or record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to any retention required by law.

7. Your Rights

Under the NDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal exceptions
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at xerelleteam@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Xerelle is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will take steps to remove it.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Xerelle may use minimal cookies or similar technology necessary for the Platform to function (such as keeping you logged in). We do not use tracking for third-party advertising purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to users through the Platform.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be directed to xerelleteam@gmail.com.
