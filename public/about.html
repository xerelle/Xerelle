About Xerelle

A private space to connect, on your own terms.

Xerelle is a Nigerian platform where verified individuals build a Room — a private, subscription-based space where members can connect with them through messaging, Feed posts, and Stories.

We built Xerelle around a simple idea: genuine connection deserves a space that's private, safe, and set up so both sides are treated fairly — the person sharing her time and attention, and the member paying to be part of her world.

What we stand for

Privacy first
No public directory, no browsing, no being listed anywhere. You're only reachable by someone who already has your exact link or username.
Real people, verified
Every Room on Xerelle belongs to someone who's completed identity verification before going live.
You set the terms
Models choose their own subscription price and keep the majority of everything they earn.
Non-explicit, always
Xerelle is built for genuine connection, not explicit content. That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation.
