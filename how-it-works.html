How It Works

1
Create your Room
Sign up with your name, a username, and a phone number — this takes a few minutes. Your username becomes your personal Room link (like xerelle.com/yourname), the one link you'll ever need to share. You'll also set your own monthly subscription price here, starting from a ₦10,000 floor — go higher if you believe your access is worth more.
2
Get verified
Upload a government ID and a quick selfie. This is reviewed before your Room goes live — it's what makes every Room on Xerelle genuine, protecting both you and the people who reach out to you.
3
Personalize your Room
Add a profile photo and a few gallery photos so people know it's really you. Post a Story whenever you want to keep your Room feeling active and current — it disappears after 24 hours, just like you'd expect.
4
Share your link and start earning
Send your exact Room link to the fans already filling your DMs elsewhere. From here on, only people with your link or exact username can ever reach you — no public browsing, no strangers finding you by accident. When someone messages or subscribes, you'll see it in your own inbox and can reply directly, on your own terms.
